The records behind the story.

Eight folios from the Archive of Selquorath: the chronicle, the chart, the Compass, the Forgotten Tongue, the bestiary, and the accounts of those who lived through the Great Calm.

  1. The Rise and Fall of the Leviathan Tyrant. In the deepest reaches, where sunlight dies and forgotten kingdoms sleep, a single pearl would change the fate of Selquorath forever.
  2. From the abyss a single pearl returned, and with it, a greater fate awakened. The king placed the pearl in the care of his five scholars.
  3. The Crown of Tides, the Abyss Needle, the Heart Core, and the Seafarer Base. Four pieces to hold one pearl.
  4. Five parts. One purpose. At last, it is whole. The Compass awakens, and something hears. Across the depths, a leviathan stirs.
  5. What began as a quest for knowledge became a weapon of unimaginable power. With the Compass, he commanded the leviathans, and the seas obeyed.
  6. We had served a king who served the Leviathan. So we met in secret, and chose a different path. We swear: to break his reign, to return the tides, to set the world free.
  7. The sea remembers its own. Pride drowns deepest. And so the tyrant is gone. The scholars move to secure what remains.
  8. With the king dead, we did what must be done, so that it would never be whole again. Five pieces. Five keepers. One hope.
  9. Five scholars. Five pieces. To the Western Reach, the Frozen North, the Golden Main, the Jade Falls, and the Crown Isles. Different paths. A single hope.
  10. Five pieces, scattered wide. The scholars are gone. Their deeds fade. Their names are lost to time. But the sea remembers, and somewhere in the depths, the leviathans still stir.
