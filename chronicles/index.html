THE SIX REGIONS

Six powers. Countless stories.

The Frozen North

Bjorn IronwolfThe Wolf of the North

Snow, rugged fjords, and blue steel. A northern realm shaped by the sea and the strength of its people.

The Western Reach

Demir KaraoğluThe Iron Pasha

A dignified maritime power, defined by disciplined fleets and a proud martial tradition.

The Pearl Isle

Omar Al-BahraniThe Prince of the Tides

Pearls, moonlit waters, and a deep seafaring tradition. A realm of refinement and exploration.

The Jade Falls

Empress Mei-Lin ZhengThe Dragon Empress

Jade, waterfalls, and imperial majesty. Tradition and innovation meet across its powerful fleets.

The Crown Isles

Lady Victoria BlackthorneHer Majesty’s Sea Lioness

Heraldic nobility and maritime authority. A proud realm of grand ports and far-reaching ambitions.

The Golden Main

Don Mateo de la CruzThe Gilded Admiral

Golden ports, mercantile prestige, and fortunes carried by the tide. Commerce is a source of power.
