Cookie Policy

Last updated: 11 September 2026. Short version: this website does not set cookies.

Cookies and browser storage

The MythWave Studios website does not set advertising, analytics, or functional cookies, and it does not use browser storage to track visitors. There is no sign-in, so there are no session cookies either.

External content

Opening a store listing, trailer, or social link takes you to another service, which may use its own cookies. This website does not embed third-party players or widgets on its pages.

Browser controls

You can review and remove cookies for any website through your browser settings.

Future changes

If we ever introduce analytics, advertising, or other tracking, we will update this policy and add the appropriate controls before enabling it.
