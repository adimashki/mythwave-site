Common questions

Is Ocean Rivals free to play?
Yes. The game is free, with no ads and no real-money purchases. Any items you unlock are earned with in-game gold you win by playing.
Do I need an internet connection?
No. Ocean Rivals runs fully offline. You can play anywhere, with or without a connection.
Where is my progress saved?
Your progress is saved on your device. It is not stored on our servers, and there is no account to sign in to. Deleting the game removes your saved progress.
Can I move my progress to a new device?
Not at the moment. Because progress is stored locally and there is no cloud save or account, it stays on the device where you played. This may change in a future update.
How do I report a bug or a crash?
Email support@mythwavestudios.com with your device model, operating system version, and a short description of what happened. A screenshot helps if you have one.
How is my privacy handled?
The game collects no personal data. You can read the full details in our Privacy Policy.
