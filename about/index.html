ABOUT MYTHWAVE STUDIOS

A shared vision. A shared universe.

MythWave Studios is an independent game studio driven by storytelling, adventure, and worldbuilding.

We’re building a shared universe — Selquorath — where each game reveals a different part of the world. Characters, regions, conflicts, and eras connect our adventures across genres.

From tactical sea battles and trading journeys to northern mysteries and playful childhood adventures, we create different ways to explore the same imaginative setting.
