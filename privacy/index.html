About this notice

This notice describes the current MythWave Studios website preview. It covers the studio website only; individual games will have their own privacy information before release.

Information used by this website

This GitHub Pages edition does not provide player accounts, checkout, subscriptions, a contact form, advertising, analytics, or an online content editor. GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security when hosting GitHub Pages.

Studio content and uploads

The pages and images in this edition are maintained as repository files. It does not accept uploads or store private news drafts. Repository visibility is controlled through GitHub.

External services

Store buttons, social links, and trailer links may take you to external websites. Images or videos configured with external URLs are delivered by their respective hosts. Those services have their own privacy practices.

Children and game information

The website introduces games for different audiences, including children. It does not collect player profiles or gameplay information. The privacy practices of each game will be explained separately before its release.

Contact and final publication

The studio’s privacy contact, relevant data-controller details, applicable rights process, retention periods, and hosting-provider disclosures are still to be finalized for public launch. Please check the Contact page for available studio contact details.
